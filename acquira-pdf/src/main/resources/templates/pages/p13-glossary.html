This glossary explains key terms and metrics used throughout your monthly performance report. Understanding these definitions will help you get the most from your data.

👥
Unique Customers

Count of distinct card numbers used at your location. One customer may use multiple cards, so this is an approximation. It provides a reliable proxy for footfall and customer reach.

💰
Total Sales Volume

The total value of all transactions processed in your settlement currency during the reporting period. This uses your settlement currency, not the original transaction currency.

📊
ATV (Average Transaction Value)

Total sales divided by total number of transactions. A higher ATV means each customer spends more per visit. Industry benchmarks vary by sector — quick service restaurants typically see lower ATV than retail, for example.

📈
MoM Growth (Month-over-Month)

The percentage change compared to the previous month. A positive value (green ↑) means growth, while a negative value (red ↓) indicates a decrease. Calculated as: (Current - Previous) / Previous × 100%.

📅
YoY Growth (Year-over-Year)

Compares performance to the same month last year, eliminating seasonal bias. If 12+ months of data are unavailable, a half-year comparison is used and labelled accordingly.

🔁
Customer Retention Rate

The percentage of unique customers (distinct card numbers) that visited more than once during the reporting period. What constitutes a healthy rate varies by industry, location, and business type. Tracking this figure over time is often more useful than comparing it to a fixed benchmark.

⭐
Peak Day / Peak Hour

The day and hour that generated the highest sales volume. Highlighted in gold on charts. Use this to plan staffing, promotions, and inventory around your busiest periods.

🌍
Local vs International Cards

Local (domestic) cards are issued in the same country as your location. International cards are issued abroad — these customers are typically tourists or business travellers and are eligible for DCC.

💱
DCC (Dynamic Currency Conversion)

When an international cardholder pays, DCC offers them the choice to pay in their home currency. When they opt in, you earn approximately 3% of the transaction value. The conversion rate measures how many eligible customers accept DCC.

💚
Performance Scorecard

The closing page provides a composite score (0–100) based on weighted dimensions: sales health, growth momentum, customer loyalty, payment efficiency, and DCC performance. It includes personalised action items based on your actual metrics. Focus on the top action item for maximum impact.

📖 Reading Your Charts
Bar charts show daily performance — taller bars indicate higher values. The highest bar is highlighted with a bolder label.
Green arrows ↑ indicate positive MoM growth. Red arrows ↓ indicate decline compared to previous month.
Heatmap intensity shows sales concentration: darker blue = higher sales, red = the peak cell across the entire grid.
